We have updated the Inference Exchange landing page and navigation to make the next step easier to find.

## Choose your path

- **[Use the API](/keys)** to sign in and create an API key.
- **[Provide compute](/provide)** to connect your hardware to the exchange.
- **[Explore the exchange](/exchange)** to see what is available.

The same three actions appear at the beginning and end of the landing page. The navigation now groups pages around these tasks.
